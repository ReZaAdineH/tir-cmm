# TIR-CMM, UTIOM and TID-CMM

The three instruments have distinct roles.

## UTIOM

UTIOM is the **overarching operating model** for security operations. Its canonical seven phases remain:

**Vision → Strategy → Crown Jewels → Threat Visibility → Threat Detection → Response → Continuous Improvement**

The three canonical pillars remain:

- Leadership & Governance
- Engineering & Enablement
- Operations & Analysis

## TID-CMM

TID-CMM adds measurement depth within Engineering & Enablement. It asks:

> **Would we actually see it?**

It measures threat-informed detection, telemetry assurance, detection engineering and validation depth.

## TIR-CMM

TIR-CMM adds measurement depth within Operations & Analysis. It asks:

> **Could we actually stop it?**

It measures preparation, authority, response engineering, containment, automation, forensics, exercising, response tempo and improvement.

## No overlap by design

TIR-CMM contains no detection domain. It consumes detection maturity through R4. TID-CMM's IR domain is the detection-to-response handoff, not a substitute for response capability maturity.

The modules are optional measurement instruments. They do not fill gaps in UTIOM, alter its canonical phases, or replace its own maturity/capability assessment and improvement roadmap.
