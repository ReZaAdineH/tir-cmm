# TIR-CMM Overview

TIR-CMM is the **Threat-Informed Response Capability Maturity Model**. It measures whether an organisation can act on what it detects, whether the right person or system is authorised to act, whether containment lands inside adversary breakout time, whether the blast radius is understood, and whether the claimed response capability has been exercised and evidenced.

It is not a model of the incident response team alone. Under UTIOM's principle that operations functions as continuous incident response, TIR-CMM measures the organisation's overall capacity to act. The binding constraint may be authority, decision latency, business-impact acceptance, containment engineering, recovery capability or validation — not simply the IR team.

## Core questions

- Are we prepared to run an incident?
- Is someone permitted to act at the required time and severity?
- Are playbooks engineered, tested and maintained?
- Do we have graded, reversible containment options?
- Can automation operate within the authority model?
- Can we establish what happened and preserve evidence?
- Have response capabilities been rehearsed under a clock?
- Are we faster than the adversary?
- Does the organisation learn and improve after incidents and exercises?

## What it deliberately does not do

TIR-CMM contains no detection domain. It consumes detection maturity as an input constraint. Detection maturity belongs to TID-CMM; the wider operating model belongs to UTIOM.
