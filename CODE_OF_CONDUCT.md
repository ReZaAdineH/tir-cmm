# TIR-CMM Community Code of Conduct

TIR-CMM welcomes rigorous disagreement about response maturity, scoring, constraints, timing assumptions and evidence. The community should make it safe to challenge the model without making participation hostile.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific.
- Challenge claims with evidence, measurements or clear reasoning.
- Distinguish observed fact from interpretation.
- Protect confidential, customer, incident and production-sensitive information.
- Attribute external research and frameworks appropriately.
- Avoid vendor spam, unrelated promotion and repetitive low-value posting.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, threats, disclosure of secrets or private incident data, malicious disruption and deliberate misuse of community channels are not acceptable.

## Security

Do not publish vulnerabilities, exploit details, credentials, private endpoints or sensitive incident evidence in public Issues or Discussions. Use `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and community.

Strong criticism of TIR-CMM is welcome when directed at the model rather than at people.
